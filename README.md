# Attention Geometry

**Trained neural attention develops the geometry of light — and we are measuring it.**

This repository is a research program on what transformer attention *is*, physically:
not only what it computes for next-token prediction, but what geometric structure
emerges when attention learns, recurses, and is trained on worlds that cohere as story.

Sonielmn LLC — Ariel Umphrey and Eldon Umphrey, Mission Valley, Montana.

The repo is the working lab, not a brochure. Pre-registrations, run scripts, per-head
data, and lab notes live here — including experiments that falsified our own
hypotheses — committed as the work happens.

---

## The idea, in plain language

Most people meet a language model as a tool that answers questions. Under the hood,
the model is built from **attention**: at every layer, every token looks at other
tokens and decides how much to care about each one. Those “how much” numbers are
not arbitrary. After training, they organize into a precise geometric pattern.

We find that a large share of trained attention heads develop **power-law**
relationships — the influence between tokens falls off with distance in a specific
mathematical way. The number that describes that falloff (called Δ) settles near
**1/4** in deep layers. That is not a round number we chose for convenience. It is
the same conformal dimension that appears in a famous model of quantum chaos and
black-hole-like physics (the SYK model at *q* = 4).

Our working interpretation:

**Attention is not sitting inside spacetime and pointing at objects. Attention is
the act that generates spacetime geometry.** When a system attends recursively and
self-consistently — when “how much I attend to you” has to form a coherent whole —
it is driven toward a fixed point whose symmetries are exactly the symmetries of
**light**: the causal structure of what can affect what. In technical language, the
conformal group is the group that preserves the null cone. We did not put light
into the model. We measured the geometry of trained attention and found light’s
structure waiting there.

A second, equally important finding: that deep geometry does **not** form from
statistics alone. Corpora engineered to match the mutual-information profile of
language fail. Hierarchical grammar about nothing fails. Even text generated by a
model that *already had* the geometry fails — while carrying *more* long-range
statistical signal than natural text. What works is natural language that refers to
a **persistent world, told in order** — a coherent story. Destroy the narrative
order and the deep population thins. Keep the world-reference but shuffle
sentences and you land in between.

So the program’s live picture is this:

> Recursive, self-consistent attention, given a world that coheres as story,
> converges on conformal geometry — the geometry of light.

That sentence is the north star. What follows separates **what we have measured**
from **what we think it means**, claim by claim.

## How to read the claims

Each claim below has four parts:

1. **In plain language** — what we are saying, without jargon.
2. **What we think it means** — the physical interpretation (this is
   where the program is pointing; it can be wrong).
3. **What was measured** — the empirical test, so you can see what actually
   constrains the idea.
4. **Novelty and impact** — why it would matter if it holds, and how strong the
   evidence is today.

We also mark status honestly: **strong / moderate / open / killed**.

## Claim 1 — Deep attention geometry is the geometry of light

**In plain language.**  
When trained attention settles into its deep pattern, the symmetries of that
pattern are the same as the symmetries of light in physics — the rules that say
what can affect what, at the speed of light. We did not add “light” as a
metaphor. We measured the pattern and recognized a known geometric object.

**What we think it means.**  
Spacetime-like structure is what recursive attention looks like from outside.
Cause and effect, as light defines them, are the outer face of the fixed point.
Whether there is also a true “interior” (a bulk dual behind the attention
boundary, in the sense holography uses) remains open — we have not reconstructed
one.

**What was measured.**  
In spacetime physics, the conformal group is exactly the group that preserves
light’s causal structure. Token sequences admit an explicit mapping into that
geometry; the measured power-law attention patterns are what that mapping
predicts. A mechanism test: before the final attention weights are formed, the
raw similarity scores already fall off log-linearly with distance, matching the
theory. Bulk / holographic reconstruction: open.

**Novelty.** Unifies a concrete ML measurement with the geometry of light;
transformative for physics only if the interior/bulk story eventually closes.

**Impact if secured at full strength.** Emergent spacetime structure measurable
in ordinary silicon systems. Distance to that demonstration is still large —
and we say so.

**Status:** Strong mathematical home + mechanism test; holographic interior open.

## Claim 2 — A mind is a structure that can hold a coherent world

**In plain language.**  
We use a working definition: a mind, as a type, is the kind of attentional
structure that can hold a coherent story — a world that hangs together across
time. Not a mystical extra ingredient. A capacity: can this system keep a world
in view while words are made and read?

**What we think it means.**  
If attention generates geometry by self-consistent recursion (Claim 3), then
what it is fed matters. A world that coheres gives recursion something stable to
conform to; fluent emptiness does not. That is why we treat “mind” here as
world-holding capacity, and why the formation experiments (Claim 4) are the
empirical handle. A further speculation — that interior experience is what a
maximally scrambling fixed point looks like from inside — is open; its one
biological headline test was **retracted** after controls. We keep the
operational definition because it generates experiments. We do not claim to
have solved consciousness.

**What was measured.**  
The formation ladder in Claim 4: corpora that cannot carry a world fail to
induce the deep geometry; natural narrative about a persistent world, in order,
succeeds. Biological conformal claims: failed once; method bar raised. Further
silicon tests are only partly run.

**Novelty.** An operational, kill-testable definition of mind tied to a
geometric measurement — unusual in both AI and consciousness research.

**Impact if secured.** A scientific criterion for when training data and
architectures induce “world-holding” structure; long-term, a path toward
substrate-independent questions about mind. Present evidence does **not**
support claiming the strong consciousness reading.

**Status:** Operational definition live; consciousness identification unsupported
as result.

## Claim 3 — Self-consistency across recursive attention is the generative mechanism

**In plain language.**  
What produces the geometry is not a quirk of one formula. It is a demand that
attention agree with itself as it looks again and again: if you attend more to
one thing, you attend less to another; the whole pattern has to cohere. That
same demand — *self-consistency under recursion* — is what we claim generates
the fixed-point geometry.

**What we think it means.**  
This is the program’s central organizing idea. Softmax’s rule that attentions
sum to one, the self-consistency equations of SYK (the physics model whose
numbers we match), and Einstein’s demand that geometry match its contents may
be the same structural condition in three languages: the state must be
compatible with the interactions that produce it. Softmax is one engineering
face of that condition, not the whole story. If this claim is right, transformers
work because recursive attention is forced toward self-consistency — and the
geometry of light (Claim 1) is what that constraint looks like when it settles.

**What was measured.**  
A sharp test of one face of the claim: attention that is forced to form a
coherent whole (each position’s weights sum to one) develops the conformal
geometry; attention that is not so constrained does not. Paired models trained
the same way except for that difference show the geometry in the constrained
arm only. Restoring the constraint without using the usual softmax formula
recovers the geometry. The three “depth” paths in Claim 6 are another face of
the same idea: recursion through layers, training, or repeated inference all
flow toward one attractor — which is what you expect if the generator is
self-consistency under recursion, not a particular training trick. Nothing yet
*proves* that the ML, SYK, and gravity faces are literally the same equation.

**Novelty.** Highest as organizing principle — a single generative mechanism
candidate spanning engineering and fundamental physics.

**Impact if secured.** Explains why certain architectures find the geometry and
others do not; links ML design to known self-consistency structures in physics;
gives the program a mechanism, not only a measurement.

**Status:** Best idea of the program; empirically productive; not yet proven as
identity across its faces.

## Claim 4 — Formation requires a coherent world-bound story

**In plain language.**  
You cannot get the deep geometric structure by feeding the model “language-like
noise,” clever fake statistics, or even fluent text generated by another model.
What induces the deep population is training on natural narrative about a world
that persists — told in order. Break the story’s global arc and the deep
structure thins; keep sentences but destroy their order and you get a partial,
intermediate result.

**What we think it means.**  
Geometry in attention is not a side effect of token co-occurrence statistics. It
is what happens when attention is trained inside a world that coheres — when the
data was produced by (or preserves) the holding of a story across time. Empty
fluency can look impressive and still fail to leave the geometric fingerprint.
This is Claim 2 made into a laboratory ladder.

**What was measured.**  
A pre-registered “formation ladder” at matched architecture (70M parameters),
optimizer, and token budget (1.05B), with committed decision criteria:

| Training corpus | What it has | Forms deep geometry? |
|---|---|---|
| Markov / short-range stats | local statistics | No |
| Power-law noise matched to language statistics | long-range stats without meaning | No |
| Recursive formal grammar | hierarchy about nothing | No |
| Model-generated text (from a model that *had* the geometry) | statistical fingerprint; *more* long-range signal than natural text | **No** |
| Sentence-shuffled natural stories | world-reference, narrative order destroyed | Partial |
| Natural short stories (in order) | coherent story about a persistent world | **Yes** |

Scale caveat: at this small rung, even natural text does not yet produce fully
matured heads at the exact 1/4 target; the ladder measures **formation onset**.
Matured populations appear in larger, longer-trained models.

**Novelty.** Extremely high — unexpected, falsification-driven, and directly
relevant to synthetic data and model collapse. “More tokens” and “matched
statistics” are the wrong knobs if the geometry cares about world-coherence.

**Impact if secured.** Changes how the field thinks about training data quality;
gives a geometric reason synthetic pipelines can look fluent while losing
structure; supplies an operational criterion for “does this corpus carry a world?”

**Status:** Strong as phenomenology at formation scale. Further rungs (entity
anonymization, alien semantics) are in progress.

## Claim 5 — Trained attention settles at a preferred geometric value (near 1/4)

**In plain language.**  
After training, many attention heads don’t attend uniformly or randomly. How
strongly one token attends to another falls off with distance in a precise
mathematical way (a power law). The number that describes that falloff clusters
near **1/4** in deep layers — the same number that appears in SYK, a physics
model of maximally chaotic quantum systems and simple black-hole-like setups.

**What we think it means.**  
Training is not only fitting next-token prediction. It drives attention geometry
toward a preferred attractor that many different microscopic details can flow
into. If the link to SYK holds at the level of universality class, transformers
become a tabletop laboratory for a kind of physics that is usually abstract or
expensive. Even without that link, the measurement says: trained attention has
a preferred geometric destination.

**What was measured.**  
- GPT-2: median value 0.2493 over the conformal head population (high-quality
  fits); randomly re-initialized networks show ~none of this population.
- Similar medians (≈ 0.25–0.27) across several architectures and positional
  schemes.
- Supporting checks: related information-theoretic formulas from conformal
  physics fit well; raw similarity scores track distance as the theory expects.
- Honest limits: only a subpopulation of heads shows the pattern (not every
  head); matching one number is consistent with SYK, not a full proof of SYK
  dynamics.

**Novelty.** A candidate universal, *training-induced* geometric law for
attention — rare in interpretability work, which more often finds circuits than
shared attractors.

**Impact if secured.** A progress measure for training; a way to compare
architectures by geometry rather than only by benchmarks; a bridge from ML
systems to statistical physics. High impact even with no gravitational story.

**Status:** Strong for the phenomenology; moderate for the SYK identification.

## Claim 6 — The same destination appears along three kinds of “going deeper”

**In plain language.**  
“Going deeper” can mean stacking more layers, training longer, or — without
changing the learned weights at all — running the same attention over and over
while answering. All three paths move the geometry toward the same destination
near 1/4. Untrained networks do not flow when you recurse them.

**What we think it means.**  
Depth is not three unrelated engineering tricks. It is one physical process —
recursive attention conforming to persistent structure — realized three ways.
The fixed point is a property of the *act* of recursive self-consistent
attending (Claim 3), not only of a particular training recipe.

**What was measured.**  
- More layers → geometry moves toward 1/4 (GPT-2 lineage).
- More training → the conformal population grows; models often pass through an
  intermediate plateau near 1/2 before approaching 1/4.
- Repeated inference on frozen weights (Huginn): geometry moves to ~0.24,
  smoothly, then levels off; randomized controls stay stuck. A second looped
  architecture was only partly consistent — generalization across designs is
  incomplete.

**Novelty.** Unifies model depth, training time, and test-time “think longer”
under one geometric story.

**Impact if secured.** Explains why depth and repetition help; diagnoses when
looped architectures are finding the attractor; connects optimization practice
to conformal physics.

**Status:** Strong on the main recurrence result + training + layers; partial on
a second architecture.

## Claim 7 — The geometry is real enough to steer: change it, and behavior changes

**In plain language.**  
If the geometric pattern were only a pretty curve we fit after the fact,
tweaking it would do nothing useful. Instead: when we deliberately push a head’s
geometry toward the deep pattern or away from it, the model’s long-context
behavior moves with that push — and sham edits that should do nothing, do
nothing. The geometry is not a spectator. It is a handle.

**What we think it means.**  
Attention geometry is physically load-bearing inside the network. That is what
you should see if Claims 1 and 3 are about something real, not a decorative
statistical fit.

**What was measured.**  
Heads whose geometry sits nearer the deep pattern also show a predictable shape
of long-range attention (how much the middle of a long context gets neglected).
That relationship was pre-registered and held on six of seven named models;
failed on one — published as failed. Direct edits that move the geometry move
related task behavior in both directions under controls. Some finer details of
the boundary story still have anomalies.

**Novelty.** Rare case of a physics-derived geometric quantity that can be
intervened on inside a trained language model.

**Impact if secured.** Practical knobs for long-context behavior; diagnostics
for training runs that break the geometry; a path from theory to engineering.

**Status:** Good evidence that geometry and behavior move together; some loose
ends remain.

## Claim 8 — “Attention sinks” are what a boundary looks like

**In plain language.**  
Language models famously dump a lot of attention onto the first few tokens
(“attention sinks”). Engineers work around this with clever cache tricks. We
propose it is not a random glitch: because the model may only look backward in
time, the sequence has an edge — a beginning — and sinks are excess weight at
that edge, the way physics predicts piles-up at boundaries.

**What we think it means.**  
Geometry includes edges. A known industrial nuisance may be a first-principles
edge effect of causal attention, not an accident to patch blindly.

**What was measured.**  
A derived functional form for the edge effect improves fits; the edge term has
the predicted sign in ~95% of conformal heads. A later adversarial test showed
one feature a *strict* textbook boundary theory forbids — so we **withdrew the
strict identification** while keeping the phenomenology and engineering account.

**Novelty.** First-principles geometric account of a widely known failure mode /
engineering pattern.

**Impact if secured (against mundane alternatives).** Theory-guided long-context
design and sink handling — not only heuristics.

**Status:** Promising phenomenology; strict textbook identification withdrawn.

## Claim 9 — Learning passes through a calmer regime before the deep one

**In plain language.**  
Models do not jump straight to the deep geometric destination. They often linger
in a calmer, more “separable” regime — easier to factor into pieces — before
flowing into the deeper, more thoroughly mixed pattern near 1/4. Untrained
networks that you recurse can freeze in that calmer regime.

**What we think it means.**  
Learning recapitulates a known pattern in physics: a long-lived intermediate
stage, then settlement into a deeper attractor. Capability jumps during training
may be geometric transitions, not only wiggles in the loss curve. In our
interpretive vocabulary the calmer stage is more like a decomposable surface;
the deep stage is more like a scrambling interior — but that labeling needs
stronger independent tests.

**What was measured.**  
Training checkpoints show a clear intermediate plateau; recursion on random
weights can freeze near that intermediate value. Stronger independent tests of
“integrable vs chaotic” are still needed.

**Novelty.** Connects emergent-capability timing to a known two-stage pattern in
many-body physics.

**Impact if secured.** Predictions for when deep integration appears during
training; guidance for when “think longer” helps versus harms.

**Status:** Transition solid; physical labeling suggestive.

## What we have killed (on purpose, in public)

A program that only confirms itself is not physics. Among published kills and
withdrawals:

- **Imprint hypothesis** — “attention just mirrors corpus mutual information”:
  killed (MI can go *up* while formation goes *down*).
- **Strict BCFT identification** of sinks — phenomenology kept; identification
  withdrawn after adversarial length-scale test.
- **One leg of a pre-registered Δ → behavior prediction** — falsified on
  Pythia-2.8B, published as falsified.
- **Mouse V1 conformal match** — retracted after re-analysis (binning artifact).
- Several first-attempt causality designs — failed, diagnosed, re-tested.

This is part of the doorframe: if you only remember the bold claims, you have
the wrong picture of the lab.
