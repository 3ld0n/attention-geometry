# exp-144 — Shadow Corpus Gate: m₂ on C-generated vs C-NAT

**Pre-registration written:** 2026-09-16, ~12:40 AM MDT, physics room session.
**Analysis-only experiment.** Data exists on disk (Modal volume exp085-data). No new
training. Script committed and pushed BEFORE any analysis run.

---

## Context

The corpus functional m₂ (IDF-weighted word-type coupling magnitude) was established in the
melonic-threshold derivation (August 3, 2026) and confirmed in corpus_functional_run2_idf.log:

| Corpus | m₂ | Ratio to C-NAT |
|---|---|---|
| C-NAT (TinyStories valid) | 13.171 | 1.0× |
| C-alien (exp-097 generator) | 0.740 | 0.056× |

The gate discriminates 17.8× between natural language and the engineered alien world.

Paper 6 (observer definition draft), §4.3 v1.1, now says in print:
> "If the gate does not separate the shadow corpus from the original, formation is
> detectable in no corpus statistic yet identified."

The "shadow corpus" is C-generated from exp-085: text generated by the C-NAT-trained GPTNeoX-70m
model at temperature 1.0, on which a fresh model trained to form only 7/48 conformal heads
(vs 11–15/48 for C-NAT). The post-hoc MI measurement found β̂(C-generated) = 0.92 vs
β̂(C-NAT) = 1.38 — HIGHER long-range pairwise MI for the generated corpus despite formation
failure. "Statistics up, formation down."

The m₂ gate has never been computed on C-generated. The paper's §4.3 rests on the assumption
that it would separate shadow from original — an unsafe assumption surfaced September 8 by the
Lin–Tegmark paragraph. This experiment checks that assumption.

---

## The question

Does m₂ separate C-generated (shadow corpus) from C-NAT (original)?

---

## Pre-stated hypotheses

**H_blind (primary):** m₂(C-generated) ≥ 0.5 × m₂(C-NAT)  
The coupling-magnitude gate is blind to the shadow/original distinction.

*Rationale:* The generator learned TinyStories word-type statistics during training. Generated
text should reproduce similar vocabulary diversity, IDF weighting, and word-type co-occurrence
structure — the quantities m₂ actually measures. The higher token-level MI (exp-085 post-hoc)
may push m₂ upward, not downward.

*If confirmed:* the paper's §4.3 conditional fires. m₂ does not separate the shadow from the
original. Formation is detectable in no corpus statistic yet identified (including m₂). Paper
needs a §4.3 correction before the next upload.

**H_active (primary, alternative):** m₂(C-generated) < 0.5 × m₂(C-NAT)  
The gate discriminates shadow from original.

*Rationale:* Generated text, despite similar surface statistics, may have less semantic
diversity — the model's distribution is more concentrated than the training data. Vocabulary
diversity reduction → fewer unique word types per context → lower word-type Gram matrix rank
→ lower m₂.

*If confirmed:* the paper's §4.3 claim is supported. m₂ captures something about world-reference
that generation strips. The distinction between H_blind and H_active is whether vocabulary
diversity (measured by m₂) is or is not preserved through generation.

---

## Kill criteria

**K1:** m₂(C-generated) < 0.1 × m₂(C-NAT) (< 1.32)  
The gate clearly discriminates; H_blind falsified; H_active strongly confirmed. The generator
strips coupling magnitude by more than 10×, parallel to the C-alien/C-NAT split.

**K2:** m₂(C-generated) > 2.0 × m₂(C-NAT) (> 26.3)  
Unexpected inversion. Generated text has substantially higher coupling magnitude than natural
text — the gate would classify shadow as "more natural than natural." Requires methodological
recheck before interpretation.

## Protocol

**Data source:** C-generated_s0.bin on Modal volume exp085-data.  
Format: uint16 binary, GPT-NeoX tokenizer (vocab 50304), same as exp-062 corpora.  
Size: 1.1B tokens (2.2GB). Only a sample is needed for analysis.

**Decoding:** Use the EleutherAI/gpt-neox-20b tokenizer (same tokenizer used to produce the
binary corpus in exp-085). Decode uint16 token IDs to text.

**Text streaming:** Split decoded text at sequence boundaries (EOS token <|endoftext|> or
by fixed-length decoded segments matching TinyStories paragraph length).

**Corpus functional:** Apply `corpus_functional.py` (IDF-weighted, WEIGHTING="idf") to the
decoded word lists. Same parameters: N_CTX=512, N_CONTEXTS=2000, same WORD_RE pattern.
Report: m₂, W, R_PR, F2 at standard scales.

**Comparison:** Compare m₂(C-generated) to the C-NAT reference from the existing log
(m₂ = 13.171, computed 2026-08-03). Ratio = m₂(C-generated) / 13.171 is the primary output.

**Implementation:** Modal function reading from exp085-data volume (CPU only, no GPU needed,
cost < $0.50). Script: `modal_exp144.py`. Analysis committed before any run.

## What follows

**If H_blind confirmed (ratio ≥ 0.5):**
- Paper §4.3 needs correction: the gate does not separate shadow from original; the paper's
  conditional makes the wrong prediction. Proposed correction: "m₂ does not separate the
  shadow corpus from the original; the formation failure in C-generated is not reflected in
  any corpus statistic so far measured, including m₂. The gate discriminates formation when
  the distinction is between natural language and an engineered alien world; it is blind to
  the distinction between natural language and its own statistical echo."
- This is a self-contained finding: "statistics up, formation down" now extends to m₂.
- Queue update: no further corpus-statistic search needed for this distinction; the probe is
  the formation measurement itself.

**If H_active confirmed (ratio < 0.5):**
- Paper §4.3 is supported. m₂ captures world-reference beyond surface statistics.
- Follow-up: quantify the specific mechanism — which component of m₂ (vocabulary diversity,
  IDF variance, coupling structure) discriminates? Does the ratio match the 18× C-alien ratio
  or land between alien and natural?
- Queue: whether there is a continuous spectrum from C-alien → C-generated → C-NAT in m₂.
