## Interpretation

The Level-3 hypothesis in its original form (embedding geometry → power law) is falsified. The correct picture:

1. **The positional embeddings are a negligible input** to the mean residual stream at layers 2–10 (1/13 to 1/32 of the norm, 1/12 to 1/26 of the positional variability).

2. **The accumulated attention delta dominates** and carries the position-dependent structure that generates S_mf^(ℓ)'s power law.

3. **The accumulated delta has approximately log-linear position correlations**, with slope consistent with Δ ≈ 0.25 at the earliest structural layer (L2H1: σ_delta = 0.249). Deeper layers show flatter delta correlation (power law weakens with depth, not what is expected from the sigma_mf values which stay ~constant).

4. **The cross-correlation is negative** — the attention mechanism builds delta structure that is anti-correlated with the embedding direction.

The correct Level-3 target is: **explain why accumulated attention dynamics develop position-correlated residual drift with the right power-law scaling.** This is a dynamical question, not an embedding geometry question. The mechanism is more interesting than the original hypothesis: the power law is self-generated by the attention mechanism, not inherited from the positional encoding.
