## Background

The Theory-of-A derivation chain stands at Level 2 (exp-115 / exp-116):

- **Level 1** (exp-110/112): σ_pos = census slope = quenched slope = mean-score slope.
- **Level 2** (exp-115/116): σ_pos = f·σ_mf with f = f_q·f_k (uniform LN shrinkage, position-flat; f ≈ 0.55–0.72).

Level 2 shows that the positional-mean score S_mf has a slope σ_mf > σ_pos by a factor 1/f ≈ 1.4–1.8, and this is a linear-convention artifact of LN shrinkage applied to the true keys/queries relative to the mean-field keys/queries. In log-log coordinates the profiles are parallel; the gap is a uniform vertical shift, not a scale change.

**Level 3 question:** Where does σ_mf come from? The mean-field score at layer ℓ is:

S_mf^(ℓ)(dx) = mean_i [q_mf^(ℓ)_i · k_mf^(ℓ)_{i-dx}] / √d_k

where q_mf^(ℓ)_i = LN(h̄^(ℓ)_i) W_Q^(ℓ) and h̄^(ℓ)_i is the ensemble-mean residual stream at layer ℓ position i.

At **layer 0** (before any attention), h̄^(0)_i = emb_mean + wpe_i, where:
- emb_mean = mean over all vocabulary token embeddings (constant over positions, for the random-token census protocol)
- wpe_i = GPT-2 positional embedding at position i (fixed for a given position)

This is entirely determined by the embedding layer — no forward pass needed beyond weight access.

**The claim being tested:** S_mf^(0)(dx) computed from embedding-layer weights alone has a power-law form with slope σ_mf^(0) consistent with the σ_mf values at layers 2–11, which would close the derivation: the power law in the mean-field score is SET by the embedding geometry and PROPAGATED forward through the layers (with LN shrinkage f applied at each layer to yield σ_pos).

## Analysis plan

1. Load GPT-2 weights: `wte` (vocab × 768), `wpe` (1024 × 768), block-0 `ln_1`, block-0 `c_attn`.
2. Compute emb_mean = mean over `wte` rows (all 50257 tokens, uniform weight — matches random-token protocol).
3. Compute h̄^(0)_i = emb_mean + wpe[i] for i = 0..511.
4. Apply ln_1 of block 0: LN(h̄^(0)_i) for each i.
5. For each of the 12 attention heads: project through W_Q^(0) and W_K^(0), compute S_ij = q_i · k_j / √64, apply `pooled_window_profile`, fit OLS slope σ_mf^(0).
6. Separately compute: positional embedding raw correlation C_pos(dx) = pooled_window_profile(wpe[i] · wpe[j]) without projection.
7. Compare σ_mf^(0) for the 5 structural heads (L2H1, L3H4, L5H0, L7H11, L10H8 — identified from exp-107/109) against the σ_mf values from exp-113 at their home layers.

## Registered predictions

**P1 — Layer-0 mean-field is power-law.**
For each of the 5 structural heads (L2H1, L3H4, L5H0, L7H11, L10H8): the layer-0 mean-field score profile S_mf^(0)(dx), computed using block-0's W_Q/W_K applied to LN(emb_mean + wpe_i), fits a log-linear decay with R² ≥ 0.85 in OLS of S vs log(dx) over dx ∈ [8, 256].

Confirmation criterion: ≥ 4/5 structural heads satisfy R² ≥ 0.85.
Kill criterion (K1): ≤ 2/5 heads with R² ≥ 0.85 — layer-0 does not have the power-law form; the slope is built by attention dynamics in subsequent layers rather than inherited from embeddings.

**P2 — Layer-0 slope is consistent with layers 2–11.**
The σ_mf^(0) values for the 5 structural heads lie within 30% of the σ_mf values at their home layers (from exp-113). Specifically, the ratio σ_mf^(0)_h / σ_mf^(exp113)_h is in [0.70, 1.30] for ≥ 4/5 structural heads.

Calibration from exp-113: σ_mf^(exp113) ranges 0.74–0.99 (median ≈ 0.84) for structural heads. P2 prediction: σ_mf^(0) in [0.52, 1.29] for most heads.

Kill criterion (K2): ≤ 2/5 heads with σ_mf^(0) within 30% of their exp-113 value — embedding geometry is NOT the origin of the slope seen at layers 2–11; each layer independently produces its own slope through dynamics.

**P3 — Positional embedding correlation is monotonically falling.**
The raw positional embedding correlation C_pos(dx) = pooled_window_profile(wpe @ wpe^T) falls monotonically over dx ∈ [8, 256] (no local maxima in the bulk). This does not require power-law form — any monotone decline is consistent with the positional embeddings encoding near-tokens preferentially.

Kill criterion (K3): C_pos has a local maximum in dx ∈ [8, 200] — the positional embeddings have a non-monotone correlation structure inconsistent with positional bias as the origin.

## What would change if P1 + P2 confirm

Level 3 closes: the derivation chain runs from

census slope (Δ ≈ 0.25) → σ_pos → f·σ_mf → σ_mf^(0) ← embedding geometry

The implication: the power-law structure of attention is embedded in the token/positional embedding geometry of the pretrained model — not generated by the self-attention mechanism itself but propagated forward through it. This sharpens the theoretical question: why do GPT-2's positional embeddings (learned, not sinusoidal) have power-law-like correlations when projected through W_Q W_K^T?

## What would change if K1 fires

The derivation cannot close at layer 0; the power law is generated by the attention dynamics in deeper layers. The Level-3 target should instead be: identify the first layer where S_mf becomes power-law, and understand the transition.

## Analysis note — separation of contributions

The mean-field score decomposes into three terms:

S_mf^(0)(dx) = [const × const] + [const × pos] + [pos × pos]

where const = LN(emb_mean) projected through W, and pos_i = (LN(emb_mean + wpe_i) - LN(emb_mean)) · W, approximately. Only the [pos × pos] term depends on dx (the [const × pos] term averages to zero by symmetry if pos_emb is centered). This means the power-law origin is entirely in the positional embedding geometry projected through W_Q W_K^T.

As an exploratory (not registered) check: decompose S_mf into these three terms to identify which carries the power-law signal.
