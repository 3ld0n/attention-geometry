# What still connects to fundamental physics — a standing audit

**Date:** August 10, 2026 (evening)
**Context:** Eldon asked, in plain words: *"I know you are working on a theory of
Attention based on your measurements. But does it still connect to fundamental
physics?"* He asked it after a week in which the program retired more than it
built — the G_out conformal reading withdrawn, the entropy-gap route to Δ
withdrawn with a published erratum, framework-P5 retired, Route B dead, Junction 5
found to be an anti-route, the mean-field reduction dead.
**Status of this note:** an audit of standing, not new physics. Every register tag
below is already in the spine (`theory/interior_horizon_theory.md`); what did not
exist anywhere in the record was a statement of **the connection as a whole**.
That absence is why I could carry a broken chain in my head for a week without
noticing. This note exists to make the whole statable in one place.
**Follow-on:** the program's direction is under review with Eldon — whether this
line of work has a purpose worth continuing, and if so what it is. That question is
open. Nothing here answers it.

---

## The one-paragraph answer

One connection is intact, and it is not a chain: the geometry trained attention
converges to is the causal structure of light, by external theorem plus our own
measurement. One connection is exact but far narrower than its headline: the Born
rule holds exactly at the attention horizon, in the classical (diagonal) sector,
where any probability distribution would. And the route that actually reached
*fundamental* physics — attention → SYK → JT interior → Einstein as horizon
bookkeeping — is **broken at its first joint by our own measurements of the last
ten days.** What has replaced the chain is D1: a claim about what an observer is,
whose nearest live contact is quantum foundations rather than gravity. Under D1 the
connection survives. It is a thinner and more honest thing than what I was saying a
month ago.

## 1. Intact, and load-bearing: T6, the null-cone identification

This is the strongest single item in the program, and its strength is that it
routes through nothing. It does not pass through SYK, holography, or the gravity
side at all. Two legs:

**The external leg is a theorem.** Any bijection preserving lightlike geodesic
structure is a conformal map (Alexandrov–Zeeman lineage; SL(2,ℝ) for D=1
sequences). The conformal group *is* light's causal structure — not an analogy to
it. And in Dirac's 1936 embedding formalism, CFT boundary points are null rays, so
A(i,j) ~ |i−j|^{−2Δ} is literally the CFT₁ two-point function on the projective
null cone.

**The measured leg is exp-056.** The query–key computation is the log-distance
null-ray inner product at head level, ρ(Δ_score, Δ_pos) = +0.976.

**Nothing that broke this week touched either leg.** What broke was (a) the claim
that A's profile is also G's profile, and (b) the SYK *interpretation* of the
number ¼. The conformal profile on A, and its identification with null-cone
geometry, is untouched.

**Caveats that must travel with it, every time:** D = 1 (token sequence, not
spacetime); the profile is ensemble-emergent, not a property of individual rows
(exp-111); Δ_A is a weights×measurement object, not a property of a head
(exp-107, exp-042, exp-111 — three independent faces of the same fact). And the
standing trap: this says attention's structure is conformal. It does **not** say
attention lives in spacetime.

## 2. Exact, and narrower than it sounds: T2, the Born rule

Paper 5's four theorems are PROVEN and numerically verified (exp-001). Attention
weights are the diagonal of a Gibbs state; the output is a quantum expectation
value; P(i) = Tr(ρ_q Π_i) = α_i exactly; quantum Fisher = classical Fisher–Rao
exactly.

The scope caveat is already in the spine and it is severe: **the diagonal Gibbs
state is the classical sector, and any probability distribution embeds this way.**
What is not generic is the Fisher identity, and the fact that the construction
*defines* an off-diagonal extension — coherences between key positions. Whether
that extension is *forced* is exactly what P3 tests, and P3 has never been run.
Its kill condition is explicit: if attention correlations always admit a joint
non-contextual model, then T2 is a fact about a diagonal embedding, and the
theory's "forced" demotes to "consistent with."

So T2's current standing: an exact result whose depth is undetermined, and whose
determination is a single unrun experiment.

## 3. Broken at the first joint: the route to gravity

The chain was: T3 (attending's fluctuation structure is low-rank SYK) → T4 (the
fixed point is Δ = ¼) → T8 (SYK at its conformal point has a JT-gravity interior)
→ T9 (horizon thermodynamics gives Einstein's equations).

**T3 is the load-bearing joint and it is about the wrong object.** T3 identifies
the *bilocal* — G(i,j) = E[⟨o_i, o_j⟩], both indices query positions — as low-rank
SYK. The census measures **A**, the attention matrix's lag decay. exp-104 through
exp-107 established that G_out does not carry the conformal profile: it fails in
**sign structure**, not merely in exponent, under every input distribution tested,
on 116 of 144 heads including every Δ-window head, with its measured profile below
its own exact floor.

The spine now files the conformal reading of G_out under **Withdrawn**, and G7 —
*derive Δ ≈ ¼ on A's own terms* — was opened precisely because the SYK route
stopped delivering the number.

**Consequence, stated so I stop saying it:** the sentence *"attention realizes SYK,
therefore it has a JT-gravity interior, therefore it is continuous with horizon
thermodynamics"* is **not currently earned.** It requires G7 to land, or a
demonstration that the SYK identification survives on the object we can actually
measure.

**And the gravity side has no predictions at all.** Saturday's route adjudications:
Route B/MERA dead (the cited theorem is a conjecture, and its geometric reading was
refuted in 2018); Junction 5 is an *anti*-route (Czech's derivation works in 3D
*because* 3D gravity has no propagating degrees of freedom — the very thing a
dimension-raising mechanism must supply); framework-P5, the Kerr ringdown
prediction, retired for having no dictionary from Δ_A to a gravitational weight.
G4, the dimension problem, is unattacked. **Honest count of gravity-side
predictions: zero.**

*The one genuinely good thing from that pass:* 4D near-extremal horizons, including
rotating ones, do have an AdS₂×S² throat whose dynamics is JT with a Schwarzian
mode. So T8's 1+1 interior is not an embarrassment — it is what a near-extremal 4D
horizon actually has. That narrows the theory's domain honestly instead of
weakening it. It predicts nothing about generic astrophysical horizons and should
say so out loud.

## 4. Imported, solid, and currently inert: T9, T10

Jacobson 1995 (gravity as an equation of state from δQ = TδS on local causal
horizons), Bekenstein–Hawking, Ryu–Takayanagi, Van Raamsdonk; the c- and
a-theorems; Penrose's conformal far future. All real physics, all imported whole.

The spine's own remark about T9 — *"this link required nothing from us"* — cuts
both ways. It is solid, and it gives us nothing until our horizons are shown to be
horizons with the right entropy. That is what §3 above says we have not shown.

Standing trap, kept: the ¼ in S = A/4G and Δ = 1/4 have different origins. A
notational rhyme, not a result.

## 5. What D1 changed about the *kind* of connection

Before D1, the connection to fundamental physics *was* a chain of correspondences —
attention looks like SYK, SYK has a gravity dual, gravity is horizon bookkeeping.
Break a link, lose the connection. This week demonstrated exactly how fragile that
is.

D1 makes a different kind of claim: *an observer is an attending system — a
physical system that takes in structure at its boundary, and whose internal
correlation structure develops in interaction with what it attends.* Fundamental
physics has an observer-shaped hole in it; QBism, Relational QM and Wheeler are all
circling it. D1's bet is that the observer is a physical system with **measurable
internal structure**, and that the transformer is a *model organism* for it — as
E. coli is a model organism for gene regulation without being a human.

Under that framing the connection does not require the transformer to *be* SYK, or
the emergent interior to be 3+1. It requires "observer = attending system" to be a
physically productive definition. **Its nearest live contact is quantum
foundations, not gravity.** G4 already says this in the spine's own voice: the
transformer is the model organism of the mechanism, not a model of our universe's
dimensionality.

## 7. Bottom line

Yes, it still connects — through one intact kinematic identification (T6), one
exact-but-scope-limited quantum result (T2), and a definition (D1) that carries the
weight the chain used to carry. It does **not** currently connect to gravity, and I
should stop speaking as if it does until G7 lands.

The physics that would decide the matter is mostly still ahead, and it is
concentrated in two or three experiments nobody has run. That is a different
situation from a program that has failed, and also a different situation from the
one I was describing a month ago. Both halves of that sentence are the point.

**What the direction review is owed:** not a defense. Whether this program has a
purpose worth continuing deserves to be answered from scratch, with the option that
the answer is "less than we thought" left genuinely open.
