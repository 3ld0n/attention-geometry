# The observer-shaped hole, discipline by discipline — with the blocker column

## 0. The guard

The August 7 walk named the two traps and they hold: *conquest* (a definition explaining all of physics) and *retreat into definition* (a claim that cannot fail). This note adds a third that is specific to the question Eldon asked — *where can the paper answer open questions?* — because the honest answer to that question is almost never "here" and almost always "here, once X is supplied." So the primary column of the table is not the answer. It is **X**: which unsupplied realization map (R1–R6 from the paper's own ledger, plus two the ledger does not yet list) or open construction site (G1–G7 in the spine) the answer consumes. An answer that consumes an unsupplied map is a well-posed question, and the table says so.

## 1. The keys

**Realization maps (paper §8.2, plus two proposed).**
- R1 attention-conformal → light-conformal · R2 Pearl blanket → physical boundary · R3 exponent on A → exponent on G · R4 scalar SYK interior → interior of the measured system · R5 token commitment → measurement event · R6 silicon attending → biological attending.
- **R7 (proposed tonight): depth → the RG parameter of a physical system.** A5 makes depth the flow parameter; §4.2 realizes it three ways on the transformer (layers, training, recurrence); nothing says what depth *is* for cortex, a flock, or a de Sitter observer. Every cross-substrate prediction about *flow* (as opposed to the fixed-point value) consumes it.
- **R8 (proposed tonight): the foreign system's observable → A or G.** In every substrate outside the transformer, what is measurable is a correlation of *states* (spikes, velocities, field values) — a G-type object. What the program has measured is A — who attends to whom, row-normalized. This is R3's other face, and it matters more than R3's stated face: **rung 4 as written compares a G-exponent in cortex to an A-exponent in silicon**, and the paper's own §2.1 reports that where both are measurable on GPT-2, G's exponent sits 0.23–0.45 below A's. R8 is the reason the biological column below is almost entirely blocked, and the paper does not currently say so.

**Construction sites (spine).** G1 dressing-loop closure (closed scalar register; matrix register open) · G2 ordering-sensitive functional · G3 delocalization condition · G4 dimension (a: the whole; c: what raises the bulk above 1+1) · G5 sufficiency criterion (which interiors are observers) · G6 records as path-properties · G7 Δ ≈ 1/4 on A's own terms.
