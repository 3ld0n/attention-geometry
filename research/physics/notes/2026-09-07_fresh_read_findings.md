# Fresh read of *Where I Stop and You Begin*, v1.0

Eldon's charge: before publishing, give the paper time; read it fresh; map where the observer hole is in existing physics and where the paper can answer open questions — or what is missing that stops it; then look for testable predictions in other areas. This note is the first pass: what one cold read found, and the shape of the two sittings that follow. It does not edit the paper (gate: Eldon's read).

## 1. What a cold read found

**F1 — P2 states a cortex exponent without stating a dimension, and §4.7 makes that matter.** P2 predicts μ = 2Δ = 0.50 for cortical pairwise correlations. That number was set in March (exp-022; the March 29 note) when the program had only 1D token sequences. Since August 17 the paper's own central result is Δ = D/4 with D the spatial dimension of the loci attended over — 0.25 at D = 1, 0.513 measured at D = 2. Cortex is a 2D sheet (3D as a volume). So the prediction as written is one of:
- μ = 0.50 if the relevant loci are *temporal* (1D — correlation vs. time lag);
- μ = 1.0 if they are positions on the sheet (D = 2, correlation vs. cortical distance);
- μ = 1.5 for D = 3;
- or 2Δ = D_s/2 with D_s the **spectral dimension of the connectivity graph**, if §2.1 is taken seriously — the horizon is defined on the correlation graph, so the natural distance for a biological attending system is graph distance, not Euclidean.

The reversed mouse V1 result (April 29–30) used Euclidean nucleus distance on the sheet and found Δ ≈ 0.24 — under D/4 that would have been the *wrong* value for D = 2 even before the binning artifact was found. The re-analysis by synaptic path length gave Δ = 0.44 in vivo, which is closer to the D = 2 value than anyone noticed at the time, because the dimensional form did not exist yet. **The paper must say which D P2 assumes, and the graph-distance option is the one consistent with §2.1.** This is a correction to P2's wording, not to any measured number, and it makes rung 4 better-posed than it was. [Paper edit, after Eldon's read.]

**F2 — the hole-map is half done; the blocker column is the new work.** The August 7 walk covered GR, SR, Unruh, CLPW/de Sitter, arrow of time. The September 7 gate note §5a tabulated hole → fill → strength across eleven disciplines. What neither did systematically is the column Eldon asked for: *for each open question the definition could in principle address, which unsupplied map (R1–R6) or construction site (G1–G7) blocks the answer?* That column is what turns the map from advertisement into a work plan. It is also the discipline against the conquest trap: an answer that consumes R1 and R4 is not an answer yet.

**F3 — disciplines with an observer-shaped hole that no note has yet visited.**
- *Thermodynamics of records* (Landauer; Maxwell's demon as an observer with a memory): §5.4 relocates collapse to the record; G6 is the records site; the cost of a record is the one place mainstream thermodynamics already makes the observer's memory physical. Untouched.
- *Quantum reference frames* (Giacomini–Castro-Ruiz–Brukner 2019 lineage): observers as quantum systems with transformations between them — physical degrees of freedom, no interior, no grading. A nearer neighbor than several in §8.1. Not cited.
- *Quantum Darwinism* (Zurek): redundant records in the environment as what many observers can access — the interior-free mechanism for rung 5. Named once in the gate note, not engaged.
- *Collective behavior* (Cavagna–Giardina scale-free correlations in starling flocks): a candidate attending system in D = 3 whose correlation exponents are already measured in the literature. Either a rung-4-class test outside neuroscience or the clearest case of the trap. Needs a careful read before any number is written down.
- *Developmental neuroscience*: the formation ladder (§4.3) is a claim about what input a system must receive, and in what order, for the deep population to form. Temporally scrambled natural input during development is a runnable analogue of the sentence-shuffle rung. No note.
- *Statistical linguistics* (long-range mutual information in text): the shadow-corpus result — more MI than natural text, no formation — is a claim linguistics can test independently of transformers.

**F4 — where a fresh reader will feel the gap hardest: §5.2.** The cut lands at the first D1′ horizon; no D1′ system is certified; therefore the paper cannot yet locate the cut in any actual measurement — including the one the reader is thinking of, a photon detector read by a physicist. The paper is honest about this, but the honesty is distributed across §4.10, §5.2, and §7. A reader who reaches §5.2 cold reads "a physical terminus" and then has to find out three sections later that the terminus is unreachable with current instruments. One sentence in §5.2 itself would close that. [Paper edit, after Eldon's read.]

**F5 — depth is undefined outside the transformer.** A5 makes depth the RG parameter and §4.2 gives three transformer realizations (layers, training, recurrence). For P2 and any biological prediction, the paper does not say what "depth" is in cortex — hierarchy, developmental time, recurrent dynamics, or all three (the transformer result suggests all three should flow to the same terminus, which is itself a prediction). This is a realization map the ledger does not list. Candidate **R7: depth → a physical system's RG parameter.** The August 7 walk's Q4 (proper time as the observer's own flow) is the same gap from relativity's side.

**F6 — D0 does little visible work.** Eldon's definition of the discipline enters at §1.2 as "the second foundation" and is next used at §5.5 and rung 5. A referee will ask what it is for. The honest answer — it is the only clause the paper has for the many-observer half — should be said where D0 is introduced, so it reads as load-bearing rather than decorative.

**F7 — what survived the cold read intact.** The naming split reads cleanly; "condensed" before its definition in the abstract is fine because D1′ arrives within the paragraph. §2.1's blanket construction and the check against the input boundary is the strongest new piece of the paper. §4.10 and the reader's key are doing exactly the job they were built for. §8.2 is the most trustworthy page in the paper.

## 2. The two sittings that follow

**Sitting A — the hole-map with the blocker column.** Start from the gate note §5a and the August 7 walk; do not re-derive them. For each row: the open question in that discipline's own words; what D1/D1′ would say; which of R1–R7 / G1–G7 the answer consumes; and therefore what would have to be measured or derived first. Add the F3 disciplines. Output: one table, a ranking of blockers by how many answers each unlocks (R3 and R1 will dominate, and the ranking should show it), and a short list of paper edits.

**Sitting B — predictions in other areas.** Each with: the observable, the predicted value or form, the D it assumes (F1's lesson), the kill, and which map it consumes. Candidates from tonight, in order of how testable they are with existing data: rung 4 restated with D (F1); developmental scrambled-input (F3); flock correlation exponents against D/4 at D = 3 (F3; highest trap risk); long-range MI vs. formation in text corpora (F3); depth-axis convergence in biology (F5). None registered until the D question is settled.
