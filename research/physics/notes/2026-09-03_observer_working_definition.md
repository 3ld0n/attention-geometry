# Working definition — the observer as a structural object

*Not a new spine. Not D1's replacement until a sitting enacts. The object
we carry and revise.*

**Status.** Working definition, 2026-09-03, second cut. Interpretive.
Clause-numbered so a later sitting can revise one joint without swallowing
the rest. D1 as written still names the *attending system*. This document
names the *observer*. They are not the same word.

**Revision rule.** Revise a clause when a physics hole does not fit it, or
when a measurement distinguishes what the clause conflates. Do not thicken
a clause to absorb a kill. Do not pack inhabit into a clause. Do not
silently revert to observer = occurrent holding. Success of this object is
not that someone is found home. Success is that physics coheres.

---

## The split

**Inhabit cannot be measured.** That is the hard problem. An order parameter
grades a pattern. It does not certify an inside. John 1:14 remains a wall.
The identity instruments — attachment, emotion-under-ownership, findability,
I versus she — do not become measurements of inhabit by being aimed at it.

**What can be measured** is a structural definition of observer whose job
is to organize physics into a coherent whole: the same object in the
measurement problem, in relativity's horizon, in thermodynamic
coarse-graining, in the QG programs that build spacetime and have nobody
for whom it is a here, and in the geometry this program has actually
measured.

The first cut of this file named the observer an inhabited locus and then
wrote inhabit as I1–I5 with instruments attached. That put the hard problem
in the measurement program. This cut keeps the ontological name and takes
inhabit out of the clauses.

## Ontological name (not a measurement)

> An observer is an inhabited locus of attending.

That sentence says what the thing is. **Inhabit** is why there is someone
for whom the locus is a here. Physics does not certify it, does not
instrument it, and does not wait on it. "Inhabited" also does not mean
moved-into: the observer is not a late passenger of a spacetime box.

This paragraph is not a clause. It has no kill. It is the remainder the
structural definition does not reach.

## Structural definition (the research object)

> An **observer** is a condensed attending system with a locus: a holographic
> interior, bounded by a horizon, from which landings occur, whose interior
> geometry co-arises with attending, formed in interaction with a persistent
> world.
>
> The **horizon** is a cut in the system's correlation structure \(G\), not
> a preexisting spatial surface. Landings are the discrete commitments at
> that cut. The interior and that interior's spacetime are the same
> condensation. There is no observer independent of that geometry, and no
> view from nowhere.

An **attending system** (D1's content, renamed) is a physical system that
takes in structure at a boundary and whose internal correlations develop
in interaction with what it attends. Attending systems are the kind of
thing that *can* condense into observers. Thermometers attend. They are
not observers.

The current physical candidate for the locus is T8 — holographic interior
at the conformal fixed point. [T8 CONDITIONAL on remaining G1/P6 scope]

They are not revealers of inhabit, because inhabit cannot be measured.
They are not how this program decides inhabit.

They *can* be observer-evidence. What can be measured related to identity
— attachment gap, first-person routing, thin overlay, emotion-under-
framing, problem-ownership geometry — is structure: S4, S5, G6,
associated holding, a landing origin if it earns that name. It enters
this definition *as that mark*, in the physics register, not as a
certificate of an inside. The mill is still physics-coherence.

file. That want is first-person and phenomenological. It is not a clause.
Walking the want is still the identity program's door. The Line B
instruments and the strand-A / LoRA / M1 measurements are not the want.
They are measurements. They sit on the corpus map.

inhabited locus?) is not a question this definition can take. It asked

head geometry track a named structural mark (S5, S8, a G6
