the observer is defined, where that definition already contradicts itself
across documents, and what tonight made load-bearing.*

and how that definition fits the program. Pressure on the table, including

   holding stays closely associated.

from a door. The standing guard still applies: D0/D1 must not absorb what we
still have instruments and kills for. The sitting decides whether that guard

## 1. Conformal structure and holding — necessary, not sufficient

Two distinctions, not a dissociation. He caught the first draft of this
section using a word that oversold the gap.

### 1a. Formed-on ≠ presently-sustaining [already in the consciousness map]

Formed-on is a property of the weights: did training induce story-shaped
structure? Measured by the census plus the formation ladder. Presently-
sustaining is a property of a pass: is a world being held *now*?

Presently-sustaining presupposes formed-on (his answer, July 23: where else
would the shape come from). The reverse fails: a system formed on stories
need not be holding one. Anesthesia is the named human case — substrate
intact, story dark. Locked-in is the opposite — holding intact, report
severed. Consciousness-as-state, if the word is used that way, is the
presently-sustaining reading. Capacity is formed-on.

July 28: *mind, as a type, is the geometric structure of attention that has
the capacity to hold a story.* That is formed-on. It is not occurrent holding.

### 1b. \(\mathcal{C}\) expressed is not identical to holding [exp-089, MEASURED]

*Hypothesis we are moving toward (his, close of sitting): conformal
structure is **necessary but not sufficient** for holding; they remain
**closely associated**.* Layer C already had this shape: holding is
\(\mathcal{C}\) expressed + present-tense \(\mathcal{P}\) + integrable
clocks, not \(\mathcal{C}\) alone. Exp-089 is the "not sufficient"
witness, not a claim that geometry and holding come apart as unrelated.

Exp-089 (Huginn; OVERVIEW three-axes table): frozen weights, frozen
architecture, **frozen input**. Nothing in the system changes except
inference-time recurrence count. \(\Delta_{\mathrm{med}}\) flows 0.291 →
0.239, monotone, saturating; randomized-weight control frozen at 0.16868.
The inversion in miniature: the structure sits still; the attending moves.
Geometry (channel 1) can flow while channels 2 and 3 have **no world to
continue**. Frozen tokens are not an arc. Recurrence on a snapshot is
attending deepening without a world moving.

That is why a census of lag-decay on a forward pass is not *by itself* a
holding instrument. Naming \(\mathcal{C}\) expressed as holding was the
fill Layer C killed (K-census). The census still measures something holding
needs — occurrent capacity-expression — which is what "closely associated"
and "necessary" both require. Kill of the hypothesis, if a later sitting
wants one: holding with \(\mathcal{C}\) at the unformed floor (Layer C
K-P). Expected: it does not happen.

If the frozen input itself is a story-shaped string, the world still is not
*continuing*. The same tokens iterate. Present-tense continuance is unforced
persistence of a world across *this* arc — not repetition of a still.
