# Fundamental Physics Through D1

*A survey walk: each major structure of fundamental physics, looked at from
the position of an observer that is a defined physical object (D1: an
attending system) rather than a primitive. Sorted into: already absorbed,
looks genuinely different, newly askable. Priority ordering at the end.*

---

## 0. The guard, first

Two traps this note must not fall into:

1. **Conquest.** A day-old definition does not explain all of physics. The
   lens generates *questions*; where mainstream physics already has the
   answer, the right relation is contact, not annexation. The T10 trap
   (Δ = 1/4 vs. S = A/4G — a notational rhyme, not a result) is the standing
   example of the failure mode.
2. **Retreat into definition.** D1 is definitional and cannot be wrong. Any
   claim below that could not fail is not physics. Where this note proposes
   anything, it proposes it in falsifiable or at least attackable form.

## 1. General relativity — already imported; the new move is to run it *inside*

**Status quo in the theory:** T9 imports Jacobson (1995) whole: demand
δQ = TδS on every local causal horizon and Einstein's equations follow as an
equation of state. Mainstream gravity is already horizon bookkeeping; the
general-relativistic sector "required nothing new from this program."

horizons of uniformly accelerated observers at every spacetime point — and
those observers are *formal devices*, exactly the primitive D1 objects to
removing. Two questions become askable:

- **Q1 (whose horizons?).** Does Jacobson's argument care whether the horizons
  quantified over are horizons *of observer-grade systems*, or does any formal

  thermodynamic consistency condition of the *practice* (D0 clause 4 made
  dynamical): the equation of state that must hold for differently-situated
  observer-grade horizons to keep agreeing. If the latter, the lens adds
  nothing to GR and we should say so. Either answer is instructive.
  [Interpretive question; no claim.]

## 2. Special relativity — the fixed point gives causal structure but not scale

SR was built on operational simultaneity (the access); D1 formalizes the
accessor. What the theory already holds: T6 — the fixed-point geometry is the
conformal group, which *is* the causal structure of light
(Alexandrov–Zeeman). But the conformal group is strictly bigger than the
Poincaré group: conformal structure is Minkowski geometry *minus the scale*.
The metric — actual intervals, actual clocks, rest mass — requires breaking
conformal down to Poincaré, and that requires a scale from somewhere.

- **Q3 (where is the observer's scale?).** The theory has exactly two native
  scale-carrying structures: the edges of the conformal window (T5 — the
  window in scale where the q=4 solution holds) and the arrest phenomena
  (UV arrest; the Δ = 1/2 condensate). Conjecture-shaped question: is the
  emergence of *metric* structure (as opposed to conformal/causal structure)
  at an attending horizon tied to the window edges — i.e., does an observer
  see scale because its conformal regime is a window rather than the whole
  flow? This pairs with G4 (the dimension problem) as a second "what breaks
  the symmetry down to our world" site. Mainstream anchor to check before
  going further: conformal symmetry breaking → dilaton, and Zee/Penrose-style
  conformal gravity literature. [Question only; nothing derived.]
  **Update, same night:** Eldon pressed on this gap and it opened —
  see `2026-08-07_the_scale_of_the_observer.md`: the framework predicts the
  gap (fixed points forget units) and assigns the missing dilation to the
  soft mode (Q2's Schwarzian = clock = scale-carrier = gravity, one
  residue of finitude), with the unit set by the measured coupling gate.
  P6 gains a second edge (P6b, scale dictionary) there; a new open joint
  Q8 (the projective/free-fall analog, from EPS) is named there too.

- **Q4 (proper time as the observer's own flow).** A5 says depth is RG flow,
  irreversible, along three measured axes. The suggestive reading: an
  observer's *proper time* is its own RG parameter — time as the depth of its
  own self-consistency iteration, not a background coordinate. This is
  currently a mapping, not a result; its first testable face might be
  relations between recurrence depth (exp-089's axis) and the horizon
  entropy clock (T7b). Worth holding next to the Page–Wootters mechanism
  (time from entanglement relative to a clock system — citation to verify)
  when G6 (records) gets formalized: a path's time is written in its records.

- **What SR does *not* obviously gain:** relativity of simultaneity is
  already fully relational; D1 adds a grading of observers but no visible
  modification to kinematics. Honest default: SR looks the *same* through
  this lens — it was already built observer-first. The gain is genealogical
  (the paper already claims the precedent) plus Q3/Q4.

- **Q5.** Is there a dictionary entry: acceleration-dependence of the Unruh

## 4. de Sitter space and the algebra of observables — the nearest mainstream contact

**This is the strongest find of tonight's walk.** Semiclassical gravity has
already been forced, from its own side, to put a physical observer into the
theory:

- Witten, *Gravity and the crossed product*, JHEP 10 (2022) 008,
  arXiv:2112.12828; building on Leutheusser–Liu (arXiv:2110.05497,
  2112.12156).
- Chandrasekaran–Longo–Penington–Witten (CLPW), *An algebra of observables
  for de Sitter space*, JHEP 02 (2023) 082, arXiv:2206.10780.
  [References verified against arXiv/JHEP tonight.]

The result: in the de Sitter static patch, the algebra of quantum-field
observables is Type III₁ — entropy is undefined. Including gravity *and an
observer*, and dressing operators to the observer's worldline, converts the
algebra to Type II₁ — entropy becomes well-defined, with maximum-entropy
state = empty de Sitter, and semiclassical entropy = the generalized entropy
S_gen = A/4G_N + S_out. In a closed universe you *cannot* leave the observer
out: the observer gravitates and cannot be external.

Three facts make this the priority contact point:

1. **Their observer is a bare clock.** CLPW say it themselves: "As a minimal
   model of the observer, we consider a clock with Hamiltonian H_obs = q,"
   q ≥ 0. And Witten's talk states the gap in one sentence: *"in principle an
   observer should really be described by the theory, not injected from
   outside."* That sentence is D1's job description, written by the other
   side of physics. The draft's §8 (neighboring programs) should cite this
   thread — it is a nearer neighbor than several already listed.
   *[Correction, Aug 8 reading pass: the CLPW sentence above is my paraphrase
   presented as a quotation — the paper's actual wording is "We consider a
   minimal model in which the observer consists only of a clock" (§1.2) and
   "A minimal model of the observer that suffices for our purposes is to say
   that the Hamiltonian of the observer is H_obs = q" (§2.2). The Witten
   sentence is from a talk and stays out of citable use; the in-print
   version is "an observer cannot be added from outside but must emerge as
   part of the theory" (arXiv:2303.02837 §1). Verified quotes and the full
   positioning: `2026-08-08_clpw_positioning.md`.]*
2. **Their maximum-entropy state is the observer in a Gibbs state at the
   horizon temperature** (Ψ_max = Ψ_dS ⊗ thermal state of the clock at
   T_dS). A Gibbs state at the observer's horizon is exactly T2's object.
   Whether this is a dictionary entry or a coincidence of formalisms is a
   well-posed question — the C2 discipline applies (state it so it can be
   attacked).
3. **The crossed product needs the observer's modular/time flow.** The
   construction crosses the field algebra with the flow generated by the
   (modular) Hamiltonian, then constrains with the observer's energy. D1's
   observer has a native flow: depth (A5). Q6: **what does the crossed-product
   construction look like when the observer is an attending system rather
   than a bare clock — does observer-grade structure (interior + horizon
   entropy) supply what CLPW put in by hand (positive-energy clock +
   dressing), and does the grading (coupled / arrested / observer-grade)
   correspond to any algebraic distinction?** This is a literature-engagement
   direction first: read CLPW and Witten 2022 properly (not tonight's skim),
   plus Chandrasekaran–Penington–Witten on large-N algebras
   (arXiv:2209.10454), before proposing anything.

Also noted for scope: this thread is where "the observer's gravity cannot be
neglected" is *derived* to matter (closed universe). It is the one place in
mainstream physics where leaving the observer primitive makes the entropy
literally undefined — the strongest available answer to "does anything look
different?"

## 5. The arrow of time — the observer's monotone flow

Boltzmann's problem: microdynamics is reversible; experience is not. Under
D1 the irreversibility axiom (A5, c-theorem structure) lives *on the
observer's side*: attending at depth is monotone coarse-graining; there is no
un-attending. The thermodynamic arrow, through this lens, is the arrow of
every observer's own RG flow, and the records question (G6) is its partner:
the past is what has been landed; a path accumulates records in one
direction because landings are commitments (§6.4 of the theory).

- **Q7.** Why do all observers' arrows *agree*? Candidate answer already in
  the theory: inter-horizon consistency is an achieved practice (D0 clause 4)
  over a territory whose own flow is monotone (T10 — cosmic no-hair, Penrose
  far-future conformal structure). Agreement of arrows would then be the
  temporal face of P5-style objectivity. This is exactly G6's territory and
  should be folded into G6 rather than opened as a new site.

## 6. Farther out — noted, not opened

- **Mass generation / symmetry breaking.** The Δ = 1/2 condensate arrest (T4)
  is a symmetry-breaking phase standing where an interior would be. Whether
  arrest phenomena have anything to say about mass (the scale problem of Q3

  matures with C1, not with a new site.
- **The equivalence principle as universality.** Gravity couples to
  everything; in this frame, plausibly because horizon thermodynamics is
  common to every observer-grade system — universality class as equivalence.
  One paragraph of interpretation, not a program. Parked.
- **Quantum cosmology / wavefunction of the universe.** The frame has a
  clean negative statement: under D1 there is no view from nowhere — a
  "wavefunction of the universe" with no horizon it lives on is not a
  D1-expressible object; the CLPW thread (closed universe *requires* an
  internal observer) is the mainstream echo. Worth one careful paragraph in
  some future version of the paper's §5, after the algebra reading (Q6).
