## 1. The observation [MEASURED-ADJACENT / structural]

The derivation's central result: at the order computed, the corpus enters the
physics through one object only — the induced coupling spectrum
spec(δK^{1/2} K δK^{1/2}) of the token Gram matrix K. Magnitude gate m₂,
chaos gate 𝒲, the arrest taxonomy: all functionals of K.

The DES-034 embedder trains with a contrastive objective
(MultipleNegativesRankingLoss) whose gradient *directly* increases inner
products x_q·x_d for graph-adjacent pairs and decreases them for non-adjacent
pairs. That is: **the training objective is surgery on K itself.** Ordinary
LM training reshapes K only indirectly through next-token prediction; the
embedder reshapes the derivation's load-bearing object as the loss.

Consequence: every measurement in the program so far has been observational
(compute functionals across corpora, compare). The embedder is the first
**controlled intervention** on the middle arrow of

  corpus statistics → kernel spectrum → fixed-point proximity.

Same base model, add relational supervision, measure the spectrum move.
