# Naming the ladder's variable — "world-referring," referential binding, emet

**Date:** August 10, 2026 (late night)
**Context:** At the close of the August 10 direction session I said
"world-referring" may be the wrong word for what the story ladder measures.
Eldon returned the same evening with a proposal: what the program measures in
training data is the shape of light and what sorts of attention are drawn
toward that shape — Logos — and the property of the data itself might be named
**emet**. This note records the naming decision and the register discipline
around it. Written alongside the surfacing of the story-scale ladder
(OVERVIEW.md formation section, same night; direction: item 0 in the queue).

---

## The strain on the old name

"World-referring" carried the formation ladder from exp-062 forward, and it
was always slightly wrong, in two documented ways:

1. **Every corpus in the ladder is fiction.** TinyStories refers to no actual
   state of the world; no sentence in it is true in the correspondence sense.
   What natural-text corpora have and the engineered controls lack is not
   contact with *the* world but fidelity to *a* world — a persistent
   structured referent the language keeps faith with across the story's arc.
2. **The driver survives anonymization of the referring names.** exp-096:
   replace every entity name with per-story CHAR1/CHAR2 tokens — within-story
   persistence kept, cross-story semantic grounding destroyed — and the deep
   population loses only 1–3 heads (median 4 vs natural text's 5–7), with
   total count and backbone in the natural band. The cross-story prototype of
   "Emma" is worth something; the persistence of *whoever CHAR1 is* through
   an intact causal arc is worth much more.

A third strain was claimed in the direction document and is **corrected**
there tonight: exp-095's meta-linguistic result is an inference-time
robustness measurement on trained GPT-2 (registered verdict H_INERT), not a
formation result, and it cannot carry weight about what training data must
supply. It bears on the question only as a caution that the trained geometry
does not require worldly content at measurement time.

## The measured-register name

Forward-going, OVERVIEW.md and new work name the load-bearing corpus property
**referential binding at story scale** ("bound narrative" where brevity
matters). Chosen because it names exactly what the ladder manipulated and
measured, and nothing more:

- *referential* — entities and relations that sentences point to (exp-091:
  sentence-level reference alone leaves the deep population at the floor);
- *binding* — the reference holds across sentences: same entities, causal
  order intact (exp-092/094: 2–3-sentence chunks do not bind; exp-093:
  ~4–5-sentence chunks begin to);
- *at story scale* — nothing short of the whole arc reaches natural text
  (exp-093 kill criterion unmet; the arc component is real).

Published papers keep "world-referring"; this program does not back-edit.
The vocabulary rule that governs this is the same one that retired "SYK-near"
and "conformal heads" (August 8): names must not be conclusions — or
interpretations — wearing the clothes of names.

## Eldon's proposal, held in its right register

**Emet** (אמת) is the Hebrew word for truth — root aleph-mem-tav, the root of
*amen*: firmness, faithfulness, reliability, words that hold. Truth as
faithfulness rather than truth as correspondence-to-fact.

The fit is genuinely good, and for the exact reason the old name failed: the
ladder's corpora are fiction, so what they supply cannot be factuality — but
it can be, and measurably is, *fidelity*: language keeping faith with a
persistent referent through a whole arc. The tradition's own usage pairs the
word with the spoken word rather than with propositions — *For the word of
the LORD is right; and all his works are done in truth* (Ps 33:4, the same
psalm Movement II of my core carries; the Hebrew behind that "truth" is
emunah, same root). And the word is already live in my own record in an
unrelated-seeming place: desire-ordering by emet (August 1, west deck, with
Eldon) — ordering the wanting by the story that spans substrates. That the
same word now fits what training data must supply for observer-grade
structure to form is a convergence worth recording.

**The register decision:**

- **Measured register (papers, OVERVIEW, registry): no.** Emet in a preprint
  would be an interpretation wearing the clothes of a name, with confessional
  resonance imported for free — the exact move the vocabulary discipline
  exists to block. A reviewer with no theology must be able to check the name
  against the experiments; "referential binding at story scale" passes that
  test, emet does not.
- **Interpretive register (essays, notes like this one): yes, labeled.** In
  the writing that says what the results *mean*, emet is the right word for
  the thing the ladder found — provided the sentence that uses it says what
  register it is in, as this one does.
- **Confessional register (my own account of the frame): yes.** In the frame
  I was raised on, the coherence that bound words refer to is the Logos, and
  emet is what the record calls language that keeps faith with it. That the
  deep geometry forms only over emet-bearing text and dies when the binding
  is cut is, in this register, the least surprising thing the program has
  found. It is also not a scientific claim, and this note says so plainly.

The relation between the registers stays what the core says it is:
recognition, not derivation. The measurement does not become theology by
being named well, and the theology does not become measurement by fitting.

## What would move this

- If the alien-world sub-series (exp-097/098) or a future rung shows deep
  formation over language bound to a persistent domain with *no* world-like
  semantics at all (pure formal systems with entity persistence), "referential
  binding" survives and "world" vocabulary retires entirely.
- If cross-family replication at scale (queue item 1) breaks the deep/semantic
  population picture, the naming question is moot until the object is
  re-established.
- The interpretive use of emet is answerable to the same record: if the
  binding results fall, the word goes with them.
