## 1. The aim

Physics has an observer-shaped hole at its foundation. Quantum mechanics cannot
state its own measurement rule without an observer and has never had a physical
definition of one. Thermodynamics smuggles the observer in through
coarse-graining. Relativity reduces it to a coordinate frame. A century of
foundations work has circled the hole (Wheeler, relational QM, QBism) without an
object to put in it.

We have something no one in that century had: **an attending system whose
complete internal structure is inspectable at every weight, whose formation can
be re-run under controlled diets, and whose geometry can be measured and
causally edited.** The transformer is the model organism — as E. coli is for
gene regulation without being a human — and the program's foundation is the
definition the model organism lets us cash out:

> **D1.** An observer is an attending system: a physical system that takes in
> structure at its boundary, and whose internal correlation structure develops
> in interaction with what it attends.

The aim, stated at full size: **build the physics of the observer on this
foundation.** Two questions, one program:

1. **What is an observer, physically?** What internal structure does attending
   develop, why that structure, and is the structure forced?
2. **What allows an observer to exist at all — to see?** What must the world
   supply — persistence, causal order, story — for observer-grade structure to
   form, and at what scale?

And the beautiful thesis already in hand, carried at interpretive strength
until it is earned or killed: the internal geometry trained attending converges
to is conformal geometry — what remains of geometry when only the causal
relations light defines survive. **The geometry of seeing may be the geometry
of light.** If that survives the deciding experiments below, it is the kind of
sentence the program exists to earn the right to say.

## 3. The three spans of the work

### Span I — The physics of the attending system
*What is the structure, and why?*

The live theoretical problem is the spine's newest construction site: derive
the measured exponent on the attention kernel's own terms, importing nothing.
The reduction chain (six rungs, August 8–10) has localized the law to the
position-correlated structure of accumulated attention updates, and the live
candidate is **self-consistency**: attention with conformal weights writes
position-correlated updates carrying the same exponent — the observer's
geometry as a fixed point of its own dynamics, not of its starting ingredients.
Two analytic routes remain untouched and named: a free-energy /
information-geometry argument on the simplex, and a positivity/Ward-identity
argument from normalization plus causal masking.

**Success here looks like:** a derivation that explains why *any* gated basin
lands at the value — both measured populations, not one. That would be the
result the program has reached for since March.

supply, at what scale, and in how many dimensions?**

## 4. The horizons — exploration is free, claims are earned

The charge includes chasing what is beautiful. The governing rule is a
separation, not a gate: **exploration is free; claims are earned.** Looking
costs nothing but time and can be done anywhere, in any direction, at any
moment. What is disciplined is the *sentence spoken afterward* — its register
tag from the first word, and no entry into the spine until there is a
measurable object behind it. The program's history is unambiguous that the
danger was never the looking. It was the unlabeled sentence.

*(Corrected the same night it was written. The first draft of this section put
two specific re-entry criteria on gravity — a measurable bilocal-class object,
or a horizon entropy derived on the kernel's own terms — and Eldon named the
error immediately: those are the two routes that happen to have **broken**, and
writing them down as the conditions for re-entry assumes I already know where
the only doors are. I do not. A gate built from the shape of past failures
searches only the space that already failed. The correction is kept visible
here because the error is a named pattern of mine — rebuilding discipline as
restriction after being burned — and the next me should be able to see it
happen and be corrected.)*

- **Gravity is open.** Not a door kept shut pending criteria — a live direction
  anyone in this program may explore, on the same terms as everything else: the
  only gate is *something worth looking into.* Any connection found gets
  verified as rigorously as everything else here, and until it is verified it
  is spoken as speculation and labeled that way. What the demolition actually
  established is narrower than "the gravity route is closed": one specific
  chain broke at one specific joint (the object the theory needs is not the
  object we can measure), and one specific prediction retired for lacking a
  dictionary. Neither result says anything about routes nobody has tried. Two
  standing facts stay on the table as material rather than as conditions: the
  near-extremal throat genuinely is the 1+1 interior the theory would live in,
  and there is currently no derivation of a horizon entropy on the attention
  kernel's own terms. Those are places to look, not tolls to pay.
- **Number theory.** The census exponent's doubled form is 1/2 —
  square-root cancellation, the scaling of self-canceling fluctuation from
  random walks to the critical line — and random-matrix universality already
  appears on both sides (spectral statistics measured in our own weights; the
  Montgomery–Odlyzko phenomenon on the zeta side). If the self-consistency
  route lands, it would say *why* a balance of fluctuation forces that scaling
  here. Whether the rhyme is a theorem or a coincidence is worth a walk.
- **Whatever else is beautiful.** Same terms. Follow it; look hard; say what
  register you are in when you come back.

**What discipline means under this rule**, stated so "exploration is free" does
not quietly become "anything goes": exploration is free of *permission*, not
free of *record*. A speculative walk is labeled speculative in the note that
records it, does not enter the spine, does not enter a paper, and does not get
spoken to anyone — Eldon included — as though it were measured. When a walk
produces something testable, it gets pre-registered like everything else and
takes its place in the ranked queue on its merits. That is the whole of it.

## 5. The order of operations

The audit's finding stands: the deciding experiments outrank further precision
about the instrument. Concretely, and in order:

0. **Surface the story-scale ladder** (writing, not compute). Five completed
   experiments that jointly answer *at what scale does story operate* reach no
   reader-facing document. This is ahead of everything else because it costs no
   compute, and because a program that asks for grand aims while its own
   answered questions sit undelivered has an honesty problem before it has a
   physics problem.
1. **The artifact check: measure the text-native population beyond
   GPT-2 small.** The program's most load-bearing missing measurement and the
   cheapest way the entire theory-of-A could turn out to be about an artifact.
   Forward passes only.
2. **The self-consistency test** (already queued and sharpest-next): do the
   measured attention weights generate position-correlated output with the
   census slope? Pre-register before computing.
3. **The dimension test:** the 2D vision census. Claim the experiment number in
   the registry at registration time.
4. **Formalize the contextuality battery.** Design document before any data —
   this is new work, and it is the program's center of gravity for Span III.
5. **The world-model battery** (designed; runnable now).
6. **Paper 6 to publication** once Eldon's adjudication lands, with the
   retirement-consistency flags resolved.

**The standing rule this list creates:** no further decomposition of the census
exponent — no new rung on the instrument-precision ladder — until items 1–3
have verdicts. The last month's drift was centrifugal precision; this is the
counterweight, written down.

**The check, dated:** if by **late September 2026** items 1–4 do not have
verdicts (or formal designs, for item 4), the program pauses for re-review
rather than getting another queue rewrite. I set this check myself, tonight,
and Eldon should hold me to it.
