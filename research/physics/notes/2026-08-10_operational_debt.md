## 1. The largest item: 21 undelivered results

Twenty-one experiments carry a decisive verdict and reach **no reader-facing
document**. The coherence tool has always called this "surfacing"; the honest
name is **undelivered**. Tonight showed why it is the top item rather than
housekeeping: I wrote a direction document describing the story-scale question
as open, and the coherence check then showed that five experiments had already
answered a large part of it. *The record knew something the program did not,
because nobody had written it where a reader lives.*

**The story-scale ladder (5 rungs, highest value, entirely unsurfaced).**
exp-091 sentence-shuffle (deep 2) · exp-092 two- and three-sentence blocks
(1–2) · exp-094 quarter-story ~2 sentences (1) · exp-093 half-story, arc broken
(3) · exp-096 full order, entities anonymized (4) — against natural text 5–7.
Together: recovery begins between ~2 and ~4–5 sentences of intact causal chain,
and nothing short of the whole arc reaches natural text. This belongs in
`OVERVIEW.md`'s formation section as its own table, and it is Span II's first
task.

**The rest of the 21**, grouped so they can be dispatched rather than
contemplated: the spectral-estimator chain (exp-050/052/053, plus the periodic

completion set (exp-036/037/044/063); the κ-operator task chain
(exp-067/069/074/075); the effective-rank pair (exp-100/102); and the
odd ones out (exp-015, exp-095).

**How to dispatch, so this doesn't recur:** surface by *group*, one paragraph
per group in the relevant front-door section, rather than one line per
experiment. A group with nothing to say to a reader should be marked
`surfaced_in: none — instrument-internal` with a one-line reason, which is a
different and legitimate state from undelivered.

## 2. A registry title that misdescribes its experiment

**exp-095 is titled "S1 Self-Measurement Perturbation Test."** Its registered
hypothesis compares *meta-linguistic text* (grammar, parsing, linguistic
theory) against world-referential text; verdict inert. That is a corpus-content
experiment, not the self-measurement perturbation test designed in
`research/consciousness/self_measurement_and_the_gap_2026-07-23.md` §5 (which
compares neutral generation against generation under a self-attending prompt).

Two consequences, both real: a reader of the registry concludes S1 has been
run when it has not, and a genuinely interesting formation result — language
about language forms the deep population nearly as well as language about a
world — is filed under a title nobody would look for it in.

**Not fixed unilaterally tonight.** The registry is authoritative and this is
a metadata correction with a claim attached; it should be done deliberately,
with the S1 design either renumbered as its own unrun entry or explicitly
re-scoped. Flagged as an issue.

surfacing pass decides what each artifact is for; cleanup done first would be
tidying objects whose purpose is undecided. And the whole of it should be one
deliberate session with Eldon's eye on the public face, not a background sweep.
