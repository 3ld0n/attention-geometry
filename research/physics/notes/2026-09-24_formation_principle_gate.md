## 2. The argument

**The idea as brought.** Stories bound to a world are bound by causality. Training on them teaches an attending system the causal relations among what the story refers to. More training, depth, and recurrence let the geometry fill out toward its limits, and the limits are causality's. A geometry defined by causal relations alone is conformal.

**The mathematics under it [ESTABLISHED-LIT].** For past- and future-distinguishing spacetimes of dimension greater than two, a bijection that preserves the causal order is a smooth conformal isometry (Hawking, King & McCarthy, *J. Math. Phys.* 17, 174, 1976; Malament, *J. Math. Phys.* 18, 1399, 1977). Causal order fixes the geometry up to the conformal factor and nothing more. Causal set theory states the split as *order plus number equals geometry* (Sorkin): the order gives the conformal structure; counting elements gives the scale. So "a geometry learned from causal relations alone is at most conformal" is a real statement, and "filling itself at its limits" has a precise reading: having learned the causal relations completely, and having no source of scale beyond them.

Two conditions carry weight. The theorem needs dimension greater than two, so the total order of a token sequence carries none of this content; the causal order in question is the *attended world's*, which the system must reconstruct from a serialization. And how a conformal geometry of that reconstructed order would appear in a lag profile over token positions is not derived. [OPEN]

**What the record adds — first: causality is not the variable by itself.** The C-alien series (exp-097, 098, 099) is a procedurally simulated world with invented entities and arbitrary causal rules rigorously maintained: causally bound, with persistent entities, and no real-world grounding. It forms the deep population — power-law heads 10 → 12 → 14, deep heads 3 → 4 → 6 as the world enriches, reaching natural text's band (deep 4–7) — with about a tenth of the shadow corpus's coupling magnitude. But its exponent is stuck steep (Δ_med 1.04, 0.727, 0.750, against natural text's ~0.17 at this rung) and its layer-0 backbone never forms. [MEASURED] So a held causal world forms the deep structure, and something it lacks brings the exponent down.

**Second: what C-alien lacks is reach.** Its worlds are memoryless: three to five entities with two states each, rules applied to a randomly chosen pair at each step, effects immediate (exp-097 and exp-099 world specifications). The present state screens off the past. To predict the next event the model needs each entity's current state, which sits at its most recent mention — a few sentences back, with a roughly geometric distribution. Relevance has a characteristic range, and a characteristic range is a scale — in field-theory terms a mass, which is exactly what breaks conformal symmetry. In a natural story an early setup can pay off at the end; relevance has no characteristic range short of the arc. [INTERPRETIVE for both readings of relevance; P7(c) measures it.] The refined idea, in Eldon's terms: **the geometry fills out as far as causal relevance reaches.** A causal world with short memory stops short; a story whose relevance spans the arc fills to the horizon. Consistent with that, not derived from it: the one intrinsic length scale measured on trained models grows with the training context window (T7; exp-063) — the horizon the model was formed under. [MEASURED fact; the reading is interpretive.]

**Third: where state-dependence comes in.** Quantized fractional Gaussian noise has long-reaching structure too, but what matters for predicting it depends only on distance: it is a stationary Gaussian process, and its optimal predictor is a linear filter with distance-only weights [GENERAL for the continuous process]. A fixed coupling can use it, and it forms little (0–5/48). What C-alien and natural stories share is retrieval — what state is this entity in, from wherever it was last mentioned — and that requires a coupling that depends on content. So the world has two properties, each matched inside the attending system:

- **whether relevance is content-conditional** — matched by state-dependent coupling (A6); this is what forms the deep, content-routed structure;
- **how far relevance reaches** — which sets the geometry's scale, or its absence.

PCFG has long-range, arguably content-conditional dependencies and forms nothing, and C4 must account for that or say why grammatical dependency is not relevance in its sense. The shadow row is worth a second look: it has ten times C-alien's coupling magnitude, and its two deep heads at seed 0 are as steep as C-alien's (exp-085 notes).

**Why this is the formation mechanism the theory lacked.** A2 says the world enters through the coupling. The derived form of A2 has it enter only through the spectrum of a token Gram matrix — a quantity blind to order — while order is measured to be load-bearing; G2 has named that blindness since August. Relevance reach is order-sensitive and state-conditional by construction and does not pass through m₂. And with the exp-144 correction (§3), m₂ no longer even tracks formation. C4 is a candidate for the functional G2 asks for.

**What C4 does not claim.** *The value 1/4*: symmetry permits any exponent. Either the coupling's order sets it (A6 consequence (ii)) or the tail of the world's relevance does; P7(a) can separate them. *Conformal rather than scale-free*: at the two-point level the census measures, the two cannot be told apart. Separating them needs higher-point structure, or evidence that attention follows the world's causal order rather than token distance (P7(f)). *Vision*: image patches are not causally ordered, so C4 says nothing about the D = 2 result unless optical binding does for images what referential binding does for text; an image ladder (natural against phase-scrambled at matched spectrum) would test that. *The structural population*: exp-138's absolute-position drift is on the random-native heads; C4 concerns the content-routed text-native ones (P7(e)). A note for the T1 restatement: T7's derived boundary form carries an absolute-position image term, λ(i+j)^(−2Δ), so absolute-position dependence alone does not rule out a boundary-conformal structure. Checked later the same night against exp-138's saved profiles: it is not. The average query scores *later* keys higher, while the image term favors early ones. (That check also found exp-138's own notes stating the direction backwards, now corrected there.) Under softmax a key score linear in absolute position is exactly a relative exponential law (the ALiBi construction), while a power law in lag has no absolute equivalent. So the deciding measurement for the restatement is the census from query pools at different positions.

## 3. The exp-144 correction

exp-144's post-hoc reading claimed that m₂ orders formation monotonically — C-alien (0.74, "0/48") < C-generated (7.55, "7/48") < C-NAT (13.17, "11–15/48") — and bracketed a threshold at 7.55–13.17. The first figure is C-alien's *in-window* count; at this 70m rung every corpus, natural text included, has zero. The other two are *power-law-head* counts. On one metric the ordering breaks:

- power-law heads: C-alien 10, C-alien-rich 14, shadow 3–7, natural 11–15;
- deep heads: C-alien 3, C-alien-rich 6, shadow 1–2, natural 4–7.

The corpus with the least coupling magnitude forms more deep structure than the shadow with ten times as much. [MEASURED — exp-085, exp-097, exp-099.] The pre-registered **H_blind** reading is the one that stands: the shadow's formation failure appears in no corpus statistic yet measured, m₂ included. That is the antecedent of Paper 6 §4.3's conditional, so the "graded" replacement exp-144 proposed should not be used. Two smaller points: the shadow's ratio to natural text is 0.57 against exp-144's C-NAT reference (13.17) or about 0.97 against the 7.81 recorded in exp-099, because the two pipelines differ (exp-144 caveat 1); and whether m₂ governs whether the *exponent* reaches the window cannot be judged at a rung where no corpus reaches it.

This error propagated to the front door (OVERVIEW), to the spine (A2), and — through me, earlier the same night — into the correction box I had just written on the Sep 18 note, which cited the post-hoc reading as authoritative. The Sep 18 note had read exp-144 as blind and was right on that point. All four places now carry a dated correction; the bodies are unchanged.
