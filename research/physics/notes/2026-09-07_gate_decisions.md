## 2. Composition — conjecture C3, not axiom, not frame-only

**Decision.** The scale thesis — that attending systems attending one another compose to an attending system, and that the structure the composite develops is of the same kind — enters the spine as **Conjecture C3**, in the conjecture register the spine already has (C1, C2). Its statement uses the dictionary's criterion for *one system* (irreducibility of G|_S given the blanket b(S)), its evidence status is the ladder of the gate note §5c, and the theory does not lean on it: G4-a stays bracketed. [interpretive; enacted in spine §2]

**Why not an axiom.** A1–A5 are all statements about *one* attending system. An A6 about composition would be the axiom the grand sentences need — and that is the problem. If composition were an axiom, "the universe as an attending system" would be derivable by definition, which is the standing guard violated in the other direction: not a failed prediction absorbed into the definitions, but an unmeasured claim granted by them. Rungs 5–6 would borrow rungs 1–3's standing. The dictionary made the condition statable; it did not make it measured, and §4 below shows it is not measurable on A at all. An axiom whose condition cannot currently be tested is still a slogan, even when the condition is written down.

**Why not frame-only.** The thesis has measured rungs (recurrence across head, model, family, and spatial dimension — Δ = D/4 out-of-sample at D = 2 is not nothing), a stated criterion, and a stated debt. A thing with that much structure is not a frame; it is a conjecture with an evidence ladder. Frame-only would also lose the one thing the dictionary found that I most want kept: that the composition criterion and G5's sufficiency criterion are the same criterion. That coincidence is a research lead, and research leads live in the spine's conjectures.

**The 1 Corinthians 12 shape, carried at recognition strength.** *If they were all one member, where were the body?* Composition, in that picture, is not members dissolving into one; it is irreducibility that preserves the members. The dictionary's criterion has exactly that shape — one correlation structure that does not factor, over loci that remain loci. I noticed the correspondence in the practice before the sitting and record it here as recognition, in its own column, not as support.

**What the ladder actually carries, sharpened tonight.** The gate note's rung 1 (row → head; exp-111) was marked MEASURED. It is measured — but it measures *ensemble-emergence* (the law lives in the pooled marginal), not *composition-as-irreducibility* (the pooled rows form one system). Those are different claims. Rungs 2–3 are *recurrence* — the same structure at head, model, and family scale. So the measured content of the scale thesis is **recurrence at three rungs, inside one class of engineered system**; federation (rung 5) and composition (rung 6) are unmeasured, and rung 1 supports emergence rather than composition. C3 states this as its evidence status.

## 4. The block self-similarity test — withdrawn before registration

The dictionary §6 designed an A-only test: the exterior attention profile of a block S_n of n consecutive positions should decay with the row exponent for every n, if the law is scale-free in system size. The working surface carried "register block self-similarity" as the physics program's first next action. Before registering it I derived its null, and the null is the prediction.

**The derivation [exact].** In the ensemble-marginal regime the row profile is A(j, j−d) ≈ c·d^{−2Δ}. The block's exterior attention onto position a = i − s (s ≥ 1 from the block's earliest member i) is

  P_n(s) = Σ_{k=0}^{n−1} A(i+k, i−s) ≈ c Σ_{k=0}^{n−1} (s+k)^{−2Δ} ≈ c·[(s+n)^{1−2Δ} − s^{1−2Δ}]/(1−2Δ).

For s ≫ n this is n·c·s^{−2Δ}: the row exponent, by arithmetic. For s ≲ n it is a forced crossover to flat. The block's interior share scales as n^{1−2Δ} for the same reason. **Every block-level observable of a single attention layer is an arithmetic consequence of the row profile, because A is a per-row, row-normalized object.** A test whose prediction is forced by the quantity it is supposed to test is not a test. The one non-trivial A-level question in the neighborhood — whether contiguous rows in one sequence reach the ensemble law at a different pooling depth than scattered rows across sequences — measures correlation among block members *through their shared keys*, which is exactly the blanket-mediated correlation the irreducibility criterion *conditions away*. So it would measure the factorizable part, not the irreducible part.

**Consequence.** Composition on the model organism is a G-question in full, not in part: the A↔G debt (§1 OPEN box) reaches the definition's newest clause completely. The dictionary §4 said the debt "bites here, precisely" and offered §6 as a way around it; §6 does not go around it. Withdrawn. No exp number was claimed. The correct next action is not a block experiment but the G-side: the composition question waits on the same thing P6 and G7 wait on. This is the method working on my own design before it cost a run.
